Aksar Poochay Janay Walay Sawalat (FAQ)

Kya AshiKit ke tools bilkul muft hain?
Jee haan, AshiKit par mojood tamam 14 tools bilkul muft hain aur unke istemal ke liye kisi registration ya payment ki zaroorat nahi hai.
Kya meri upload ki gayi tasweer ya PDF aap ke server par save hoti hai?
Hargiz nahi. AshiKit ke tamam image tools, PDF merge aur CV maker 100% client-side chalte hain. Aap ki files aap ke browser mein rehti hain aur internet par kisi server par upload nahi hotin.
Agar mujhe kisi naye tool ki zaroorat ho to kya main tajweez de sakta hoon?
Bilkul! Hum users ki ray aur mashwaron ka intehai khair-muqaddam karte hain. Aap upar diye gaye email ya form ke zariye apna idea share kar sakte hain.
