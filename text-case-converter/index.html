Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

Title Case aur Sentence case mein kya farq hai?

Title Case mein har lafz ka pehla harf bada hota hai, jabke Sentence case mein sirf jumlay ka pehla harf bada hota hai.

camelCase aur snake_case kis liye istemal hote hain?

Ye programming aur coding mein variables, file names aur database columns ke liye istemal hote hain.

Kya Caps Lock ghalti se on hone par text theek ho sakta hai?

Jee haan, agar sara text CAPITAL mein likh gaya ho to 'Sentence case' dabane se wo normal jumlon mein theek ho jata hai.

Kya lambi paragraphs par kaam karta hai?

Jee haan, aap lakhoon haroof par mushtamil kitabein ya articles foran transform kar sakte hain.
