Har achi naukri (job) ki taraf pehla aur bunyadi qadam aik saaf suthra aur muta’asir-kun CV (Curriculum Vitae ya Resume) hota hai. Pakistan mein taqreeban har HR manager ya recruiter aik CV ko daikhne mein sirf 6 se 10 second lagata hai. Agar aap ka CV ghalat format mein hai, bohot lamba hai ya us mein zaroori maloomat wazeh nahi hain, to behtareen salahiyat hone ke bawajood interview call aane ke imkanat intehai kam ho jatay hain.

Pehle logon ko CV banwane ke liye computer shops ya internet cafes jana padta tha jahan 200 se 500 rupay kharch hotay thay. Magar ab aap apne mobile phone ke zariye ghar bethay bilkul muft aur chand minute mein aik standard A4 professional CV tayyar kar sakte hain.

1. Professional CV Ke Zaroori Hissay

Aik standard international format CV mein yeh sections tarteeb war hone chahiye:

  1. Personal Information (Rabt-e-Maloomat): Poora naam (bold aur bara), active mobile number (WhatsApp wala), professional email address (maslan firstname.lastname@gmail.com na ke koi ajeeb nickname), aur shehar/country ka naam.
  2. Career Objective / Summary (Peshawarana Khulasa): Do se teen jumlon ka mukhtasar pegham jahan aap batayein ke aap ki taqat kya hai aur aap company ke liye kya kar sakte hain.
  3. Work Experience (Tajarba): Sab se aakhri ya mojooda job sab se upar (Reverse Chronological Order). Company ka naam, designation, duration aur 2 se 3 bullet points mein aap ki kamyabiya (achievements).
  4. Education (Taleemi Qabliyat): Degree ka naam, board ya university aur pass karne ka saal.
  5. Skills (Hunar): Technical aur soft skills (maslan MS Office, Graphic Design, Communication, Sales Management).

2. Career Objective Likhne Ka Kamyab Tarika

Bohot se log internet se purana objective copy kar letay hain jo har recruiter hazaron baar parh chuka hota hai. Behtar yeh hai ke aap specific baat karein:

Achhi Misaal: "Motivated Commerce graduate with 2+ years of experience in retail inventory management and accounting software. Seeking to leverage analytical and organizational skills as an Accounts Officer at ABC Company."

3. CV Banate Waqt 4 Badi Ghaltiyan Jin Se Bachein

  • Spelling Aur Grammar Ki Ghaltiyan: Interviewer sab se pehle imla ki ghalti par notice leta hai. Hamesha final save karne se pehle 2 martaba proof-read karein.
  • Bohot Zyada Safhay (Pages): Agar aap ka tajarba 5 saal se kam hai to aap ka CV sirf 1 safhay (Single Page) par mushtamil hona chahiye. Ziyada lamba CV koi nahi parhta.
  • Fazool Shakhsi Maloomat: Marital status, religion, cast ya CNIC number likhna aam international standards mein zaroori nahi hota jab tak company ne khud talab na kiya ho.
  • PDF Format Ki Jagah Word File: Hamesha apna resume PDF format mein share karein. Word file mukhtalif phones par kholne se formatting kharab ho jati hai, jab ke PDF har device par aik jaisa rehta hai.
