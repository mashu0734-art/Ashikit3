Aaj kal jab koi shakhs car financing, motorcycle installment, home loan ya personal loan lene ka irada karta hai, to bank ya financing company ki taraf se jo lafz sab se ziyada sunne ko milta hai woh hai "EMI". Bohot se aam afrad is lafz aur iske hisab kitab se nawaqif hotay hain jis ki wajah se woh yeh nahi andaza laga paate ke asleeyat mein woh asal raqam (Principal) ke muqablay mein kitna munafa ya markup ada kar rahe hain.

Is mazmoon mein hum asan alfaz mein samjhein ge ke EMI ka matlab kya hai, is ka mathematical formula kaise kaam karta hai aur aap apna financial plan banate waqt kis tarah bachat kar sakte hain.

EMI Ka Matlab Kya Hai? (What is EMI?)

EMI dar-asal "Equated Monthly Installment" ka mukhaffaf (abbreviation) hai. Is ka urdu tarjuma "Barabar Mahana Kisht" hota hai.

Yeh woh muqarrar raqam hoti hai jo qarz lene wala (Borrower) har mahine ki aik tay shuda tareekh ko bank ya financial institution ko ada karta hai jab tak poora loan chukta na ho jaye. Har EMI ke andar do mukhtalif hissay shamil hotay hain:

  • Principal Component (Asal Raqam): Loan ki bunyadi raqam ka aik hissa jo wapas ja raha hota hai.
  • Interest / Markup Component (Sood ya Munafa): Bank ka mahana munafa jo baaqi mandah qarz par charge hota hai.

EMI Calculate Karne Ka Formula

Dunya bhar ke commercial aur Islamic banks reducing balance method ke tehat mandarja zail universal formula istemal karte hain:

E = P × r × (1 + r)^n / [ (1 + r)^n - 1 ]

Jahan:

  • E = Equated Monthly Installment (Aap ki mahana kisht)
  • P = Principal Amount (Kul qarz ki raqam jo aap ne li)
  • r = Monthly Interest Rate (Salana sharah sood ko 12 se taqseem kar ke 100 se divide karein, yani Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • n = Total Tenure in Months (Kishtoun ki kul tadaad, maslan 3 saal ke liye 36 mahine)

Amortization: Shuru Mein Interest Ziyada Kyun Hota Hai?

Aksar log yeh dekh kar pareshan hotay hain ke shuru ke saalon mein unki kisht ka zyada hissa interest mein chala jata hai aur asal qarz bohot aahista kam hota hai. Isay banking istilah mein Loan Amortization kehte hain.

Kyunke shuru ke mahinon mein aap ka baaqi mandah qarz (Outstanding Balance) sab se ziyada hota hai, is liye bank ka monthly interest bhi zyada banta hai. Jaise jaise waqt guzarta hai aur principal kam hota hai, interest ka share ghat'ta jata hai aur asal raqam ki wapsi taiz ho jati hai.

Loan Lene Se Pehle Zaroori Hidayat

  1. Tenure (Muddad) Ka Intekhab: Muddad jitni lambi hogi (maslan 5 ya 7 saal), aap ki mahana kisht utni choti hogi, magar kul ada shuda interest bohot zyada ho jaye ga. Agar aap ki aamadani ijazat de to hamesha kam muddad (Short Tenure) chunein.
  2. Hidden Charges Ka Pata Karein: Processing fee, insurance, documentation charges aur pre-payment penalty ko zaroor madd-e-nazar rakhein.
  3. Down Payment Zyada Dein: Gari ya ghar khareedte waqt koshish karein ke 20% ya 30% se ziyada down payment dein taake loan amount aur monthly kisht dono control mein rahein.
