Phonetic Transliteration Kaise Kaam Karti Hai?

Phonetic conversion ka bunyadi asool aawaz (sound matching) par mabni hota hai. Jab aap koi lafz type kar ke space bar dabate hain to system us lafz ke Roman spelling ka jaiza leta hai:

  • Aawazon Ki Mutabaqat: Har English letter ko Urdu ke makhsoos harf se jora jata hai. Maslan "k" se "ک", "m" se "م", "sh" se "ش", aur "kh" se "خ".
  • Lafzi Lughat (Dictionary Lookup): Aksar mukhtalif lafzon ki aawazein aik jaisi hoti hain magar unka imla farq hota hai (jaise "hal" se "حل" ya "ہل"). Smart conversion engines aksar istemal hone walay rozmarrah alfaaz ko tarjeeh dete hain.
  • Real-time Replacement: Jaisi hi aap space bar ya enter dabate hain, English lafz pichay se replace ho kar Urdu lafz ban jata hai.

Durust Typing Ke Zaroori Tips

Agar aap chahte hain ke typing ke doraan ghaltiyan kam se kam hon aur aap ko baar baar backspace na dabana paday, to in ahem baaton ka khayal rakhein:

  • Choti 'h' aur Bari 'h': Aam "hai" ya "hum" likhne ke liye simple h likhein (ہ). Agar aap ko "Muhammad" ya "Ahmad" likhna ho to aksar H (capital H) istemal hota hai (ح).
  • Ain (ع) aur Hamza (ء): Alfaaz jaise "aam" (عام) ya "ilm" (علم) ke liye shuru mein e ya capital A ka ihtimam kijiye.
  • Do-Chashmi Hay (ھ): Bhaari aawazon ke liye harf ke sath h milayein, jaise "bhai" → بھائی، "ghar" → گھر، "khol" → کھول.
  • Noon Ghunna (ں): Lafz ke aakhir mein 'n' likhne par aksar noon ghunna banta hai jaise "hain" (ہیں) ya "maan" (ماں).
