Zakat Islam ka aik azeem-ush-shaan rukan hai jo har sahib-e-istita'at (sahib-e-nisab) musalman par saal mein aik martaba ada karna farz hai. Yeh mehaz aik khairat nahi balkay maashray mein daulat ki munsifana taqseem aur maal ko paak karne ka ilahi nizaam hai. Lekin har saal Ramzan-ul-Mubarak ya saal ke ikhtitam par bohot se log is uljhan mein mubtala ho jatay hain ke Zakat ka sahi hisab kaise lagaya jaye, kon se amwal par Zakat banti hai aur udhar ya qarz ko kaise minus kiya jaye.

Is tafseeli rehnuma mein hum asan Roman Urdu mein wazahat karenge ke Shar'i nisab kya hai aur aap apne maal ka hisab kaise khud ba-aasani laga sakte hain.

1. Zakat Ka Nisab Kya Hai? (Gold & Silver Threshold)

Nisab woh kam az kam had (threshold) hai jis par pohnchne ke baad kisi musalman par Zakat wajib hoti hai:

  • Sona (Gold): 7.5 tola (taqreeban 87.48 grams). Agar kisi ke paas sirf sona ho aur koi chandi ya naqd raqam na ho, to 7.5 tolay sona hone par hi Zakat farz hoti hai.
  • Chandi (Silver): 52.5 tola (taqreeban 612.36 grams). Jumhoor fuqaha ke mutabiq, jab kisi shakhs ke paas sona aur chandi dono hon, ya naqd raqam aur tijarti samaan ho, to chandi ke nisab (52.5 tolay) ki mojooda qeemat ko bunyad banaya jata hai taake ghareebon aur mustahiqqeen ka ziyada faida ho sakay.

Aam tor par Pakistan mein 52.5 tolay chandi ki qeemat State Bank ya mehkama auqaf har saal Ramzan ke aaghaz mein notify karta hai. Agar aap ke kul zakatable amwal is qeemat ke barabar ya us se zayed hain, to aap par Zakat farz ho chuki hai.

2. Kon Se Amwal Par Zakat Wajib Hoti Hai?

Apni Zakat ka hisab lagate waqt mandarja zail amwal ki mojooda market value jama karein:

  1. Sona Aur Zewarat: Tamam sonay ke zewarat, biscuits, coins waghera ki mojooda market price (tola ya gram ke hisab se). Chahay zewar istemal mein ho ya tijarat ke liye, aksar ahnaaf ke nazdeek sonay par Zakat farz hai.
  2. Chandi Ke Bartan Ya Zewar: Chandi ke zewarat ya coins ki mojooda qeemat.
  3. Naqd Raqam (Cash in Hand & Bank Accounts): Ghar mein mojood currency notes, bank ke current accounts, savings accounts, Prize Bonds aur foreign currency ki kul value.
  4. Maal-e-Tijarat (Business Stock): Agar aap ki dukan ya karobar hai, to sale ke liye mojood samaan ki wholesale khareed qeemat (purchase value). Karobari factory, dukan ki imarat ya machine par Zakat nahi hoti, balkay sirf saleable inventory par hoti hai.
  5. Wusool Talab Qarzay (Receivables): Woh raqam jo aap ne kisi ko udhar di ho aur us ki wusooli ki qawi umeed ho.

3. Kon Si Cheezon Par Zakat Nahi Hoti?

Zati istemal ki bunyadi ashya (Hajat-e-Asliya) par Zakat nahi hoti:

  • Rehaish ka makan ya plot (jis par ghar banana maqsood ho, nafr-e-tijarat na ho).
  • Zati istemal ki gari ya motorcycle.
  • Ghar ka zati furniture, electronic ashya, kapray aur bartan.
  • Daftar ya dukan ki permanent fittings, computers aur machines jo samaan banane mein istemal hoti hon.

4. Wajib-ul-Ada Qarz (Liabilities) Ko Manfi Karna

Kul amwal jama karne ke baad, woh qarzay minus karein jo fori tor par wajib-ul-ada hain:

Maslan dukan walon ka baki udhar, utility bills jo baaqi hon, ya kisi fard se liya gaya qarz jo foran ada karna hai. Inhein manfi karne ke baad jo raqam bache gi, usay Net Zakatable Assets kaha jata hai.

5. Zakat Calculate Karne Ka 2.5% Formula

Agar Net Zakatable Amount chandi ke nisab ke barabar ya us se zyada hai, to us par 2.5% (yani kul raqam ka 40wan hissa) Zakat ada ki jaye gi:

Zakat Formula = Net Zakatable Assets × 0.025 (ya Net Assets ÷ 40)

Misal ke tor par: Agar tamam amwal jama kar ke aur qarz nikaal kar net raqam 400,000 PKR banti hai, to Zakat hogi: 400,000 / 40 = 10,000 PKR.

Mustahiqqeen (Masarif-e-Zakat)

Quran-e-Majeed ki Surah At-Taubah (Aayat 60) mein Zakat ke 8 masarif bayan kiye gaye hain jin mein sab se pehle fuqara aur masakeen shamil hain. Zakat apne walidain, dada, dadi, aulaad, potay poti ya miyan-biwi aik doosray ko nahi de sakte. Lekin mustahiq bhai, behen, chacha, phuphi ya ghareeb parosi ko dena zyada afzal hai kyunke is mein sil-e-rehmi ka dohra ajar hai.
