Kya main Urdu mein bhi CV bana sakta hoon?

Jee haan, hamara preview Urdu rasm-ul-khat aur English dono ko mukammal support karta hai.

Kya CV maker bilkul free hai?

Jee haan, 100% muft hai aur koi watermark ya subscription charge nahi hota.
