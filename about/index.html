Hamare Baare Mein (About AshiKit)

AshiKit aik aazadana, muft aur privacy-first web utilities platform hai jo Urdu aur Roman Urdu bolne walon ke liye banaya gaya hai.

Hamara Maqsad (Our Mission)

Pakistan aur dunya bhar mein lakhoon log rozana apne mobile phones par Roman Urdu (English letters) mein baat karte hain. Magar jab unhein authentic Nastaliq Urdu rasm-ul-khat likhna ho, Zakat ka hisab lagana ho, monthly bank installment maloom karni ho ya documents aur CV tayyar karne hon to unhein mushkil software ya paid apps par munhasir hona padta hai.

AshiKit ka maqsad in tamam rozmarrah ke kaamon ko aik hi platform par, bilkul muft aur baghair kisi login ya app install kiye hal karna hai. Hamara har tool seedha aap ke web browser mein chalta hai.

Ye Site Kaun Chalata Hai? (Who Runs the Site)

AshiKit ko passionate Pakistani front-end developers aur designers ki aik choti team chalati hai jo Urdu zaban aur digital technology ke darmiyan faslay ko kam karne ke liye pur-azm hain. Hum mukammal tor par standard vanilla web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) istemal karte hain taake site mobile par nihayat tezi se khulay.

AshiKit Kya Offer Karta Hai? (What We Offer)

Hamari platform par 14 specialized muft tools dastiyab hain:

  • Text & Language: Roman Urdu to Urdu Converter, Word & Character Counter, Text Case Converter, Number to Urdu Words, Stylish Name Generator, aur Shayari Card Maker.
  • Calculators & Finance: Shar'i Zakat Calculator (silver nisab ke sath), EMI & Installment Calculator, aur Age Calculator.
  • Media & Documents: Private Image Compress, Image Resize, Client-side PDF Merge, Professional CV Maker, aur QR Code Generator.

Ye Service Bilkul Free Kyun Hai? (Why It Is Free)

Humara yaqeen hai ke basic digital tools har Pakistani shehri ke liye muft aur dastiyab hone chahiye. AshiKit ke servers aur domain ke akhrajat Google AdSense advertisements ke zariye poore kiye jate hain. Hum user experience ko kharab kiye baghair standard ads chalate hain taake kisi bhi user se koi subscription fee ya subscription charge na li jaye.

100% Client-Side Privacy Ka Waada

Aap ki files aur data hamari pehli tarjeeh hain. Hamare image tools (Compress, Resize), PDF merge tool aur CV maker aap ke kisi bhi document ya tasveer ko hamare kisi server par upload nahi karte. Tamam processing aap ke phone ya computer ki local memory mein hoti hai.
