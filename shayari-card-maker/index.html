Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

Kya card par Urdu Nastaliq font theek dikhta hai?

Jee haan, hum Noto Nastaliq Urdu font use karte hain jo canvas par nihayat khoobsurat aur wazeh nazar aata hai.

WhatsApp Status ke liye konsa size behtar hai?

WhatsApp Status aur TikTok stories ke liye 9:16 Portrait ratio muntakhib karein.

Instagram Post ke liye konsa size hona chahiye?

Instagram post aur Facebook ke liye 1:1 Square ratio behtareen rehta hai.

Kya main shair ka naam bhi shamil kar sakta hoon?

Jee haan, Poet/Author wale box mein shair ka naam likhein, wo card ke neeche golden rang mein aa jaye ga.
