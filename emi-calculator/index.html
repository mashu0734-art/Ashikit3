EMI & Installment Calculator

Qist ka andaza lagayein. Gaari, bike, personal loan ya home financing ki mahana qist aur kul markup maloom karein.

📊 EMI Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? EMI ka matlab hai Equated Monthly Installment (har mahine ada ki jane wali mustaqil raqam). Aap ne jitna loan liya hai (Principal Raqam), bank ka saalana markup rate (jaise 14% ya 18%), aur kitne mahinon mein wapis karna hai (Muddat) darj karein. Calculator foran aap ko Mahana Qist, Kul Adaigi aur Extra Raqam (Profit/Markup) hisab kar ke bataye ga.
Aam Muddat:

Formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]. Bank processing fees ya insurance is mein shamil nahi hain.

Kaise Istemal Karein (How to Use)

  1. Qarz Ki Raqam Likhein: Jitni raqam aap financing par le rahe hain wo pehle box mein likhein.
  2. Markup Rate Likhein: Bank ka saalana interest ya profit rate darj karein (jaise 15%).
  3. Mahinon Ki Tadaad: Jitne mahinon mein loan wapis karna hai wo muddat chunein (jaise 36 mahine).
  4. Nateeja Dekhein: "Hisab Lagayein" dabane par foran mahana qist aur kul munafa zahir hoga.

EMI Calculator Ki Mukammal Wazahat

Jab bhi koi shakhs gaari, bike, ghar ya karobar ke liye kisi bank ya leasing company se qarz leta hai to sab se pehla sawal ye hota hai ke har mahine kitni qist ada karni hogi aur kul kitna izafi paisa (profit ya interest) dena padega. Aam tor par log andaza lagane mein ghalti kar bethte hain kyunke compound interest ka hisab saadha nahi hota.

Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

EMI ka kya matlab hota hai?

EMI ka matlab Equated Monthly Installment hai yani wo aik jaisi mahana qist jo qarz ada karne ke liye har mahine di jati hai.

Kya ye calculator Islamic banking par lagu hota hai?

Jee haan, Islamic car ya home financing mein jahan saalana profit rate tay hota hai, ye formula wahan bhi barabar kaam karta hai.

Muddat mahinon mein likhna zaroori hai?

Jee haan, agar 1 saal ka loan hai to 12 mahine, aur agar 3 saal hain to 36 mahine likhein.

Extra amount ka kya matlab hai?

Extra amount asal qarz (principal) ke upar bank ka kul markup ya sood hota hai jo poori muddat mein ada hota hai.
