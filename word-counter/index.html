Word Counter Ki Mukammal Wazahat

Taleemi maidan mein talba o talibaat ko aksar mukhtalif mazameen (essays), assignments ya thesis likhte waqt alfaz ki aik makhsoos hadd (misal ke tor par 500 ya 1000 alfaz) ka paband hona padta hai. Isi tarha content writers, bloggers aur social media managers ko bhi search engine optimization (SEO) aur platform limits (jaise Meta title ya Twitter X post) ke mutabiq lafzon aur haroof ka hisab rakhna padta hai.

AshiKit Word & Character Counter khas tor par Pakistani users ki zarooriyat ke mutabiq design kiya gaya hai jo English ke sath sath Roman Urdu aur Nastaliq Urdu script dono ko yakja tor par handle karta hai. Ye tool na sirf alfaz aur haroof balkay jumlon, paragraphs aur taqreeban parhne ke waqt ka bhi hisab nikal kar deta hai. Sab kuch real-time aur private tareeqe se hota hai.

Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

Kya ye counter Urdu rasm-ul-khat ke alfaz sahi count karta hai?

Jee haan, hamara counter Urdu ki khas space aur alamaat (jaise comma ، aur khatma ۔) ko pehchan kar durust hisab lagata hai.

Characters with space aur without space mein kya farq hai?

With space mein khali jagahon (spaces) ko bhi aik character mana jata hai, jabke without space mein sirf asal haroof count hote hain.

Reading time ka hisab kaise lagaya jata hai?

Aam tor par aik insan 200 alfaz fi minute ki raftaar se parhta hai, is bunyad par reading time ka andaza hota hai.

Kya koi word limit ya pabandi hai?

Nahi, aap jitna chahein lamba mazmoon ya kitab ka chapter paste kar ke foran hisab laga sakte hain.
