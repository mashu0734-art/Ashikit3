Kaise Istemal Karein (How to Use)

  1. Date of Birth Select Karein: Calendar se apna saal, mahina aur paidaish ka din muntakhib karein.
  2. Reference Date Check Karein: "Kis tareekh tak" mein aam tor par aaj ki tareekh khud ba khud aati hai.
  3. Button Dabayein: "Umar Maloom Karein" par click karein.
  4. Detailed Stats Dekhein: Saal, mahine, din, kul ghante, aglay birthday ka din aur apna astrological star dekhein.

Age Calculator Ki Mukammal Wazahat

Zindagi ke mukhtalif marahal mein humein apni exact umar ki zaroorat padti hai, maslan job application form bharte waqt jahan saal, mahine aur din alag alag likhne hote hain (e.g. 24 Saal, 3 Mahine, 12 Din). Isi tarha passport banwate waqt, driving license ya kisi sarkari imtehan (CSS, PMS, ISSB) ki age eligibility criteria check karne ke liye bhi exact calculation chahiye hoti hai.

AshiKit Age Calculator aap ko 100% calendar-accurate calculations muhayya karta hai jo leap years (366 din wale saal) aur har mahine ke 30/31 dino ka khayal rakhta hai. Mazeed ye ke ye tool aap ko dilchasp maloomat bhi deta hai jaise ke aap ne zameen par kitne ghante guzar liye hain aur aap ka Agla Birthday kis din aur kitne dino baad aane wala hai.

Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

Age calculate karne ka sahi tareeqa kya hai?

Apni date of birth (paidaish ki tareekh, mahina aur saal) select karein aur Hisab Lagayein button dabayein.

Kya ye tool leap year (leap saal) ko count karta hai?

Jee haan, calendar ke tamam 365 aur 366 dino ka leap year ke mutabiq 100% accurate hisab hota hai.

Agli saalgirah ka pata kaise chalta hai?

Calculator aaj ki tareekh aur aap ki paidaish ki tareekh ka mawazna kar ke baaqi din aur din ka naam batata hai.

Kya kisi makhsoos maazi ki tareekh par bhi umar nikaali ja sakti hai?

Jee haan, 'Kis Tareekh Tak' wale box mein koi bhi maazi ya mustaqbil ki tareekh set ki ja sakti hai.
