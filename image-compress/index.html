Image Compression Ki Mukammal Wazahat

Aaj kal ke smart phones ke cameras high megapixel photos banate hain jin ka size aam tor par 5MB se 15MB tak chala jata hai. Itni bhari tasweer internet par upload karna mushkil hota hai, khas tor par jab aap kisi sarkari nokri (NTS, FPSC, PPSC) ya university admission portal par form bhar rahe hon jahan 100 KB ya 200 KB ki pabandi hoti hai.

AshiKit Image Compress tool is baat ko yaqeeni banata hai ke aap ka data aur privacy 100% mehfooz rahe. Hum HTML5 Canvas client-side technology use karte hain, jis ka matlab ye hai ke aap ki photo internet ke zariye kisi server par upload nahi hoti balkay aap ke browser ke andar hi pixel compression perform hoti hai. Is se aap ka mobile internet data bhi bachta hai aur file secondon mein compress ho kar download ke liye tayyar ho jati hai.

Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

Kya meri tasweer kisi server par upload hoti hai?

Hargiz nahi! AshiKit par tamam compression aap ke phone ya computer ke browser mein hoti hai. Koi file upload nahi hoti.

Konsi quality sab se behtareen rehti hai?

70% standard recommended quality hai jis se file size bohot kam ho jata hai aur photo bilkul saaf rehti hai.

Kya PNG aur JPG dono compress ho sakte hain?

Jee haan, JPG, PNG aur WebP formats ko asani se compress kiya ja sakta hai.

Govt job ya admission form ke liye size kaise kam karein?

Photo chunein aur slider ko 60% ya 50% par karein jab tak naya size 200 KB ya matlooba hadd se kam na ho jaye.
