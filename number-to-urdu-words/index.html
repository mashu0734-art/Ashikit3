Ahem Sawalat (FAQs)

Bank cheque likhte waqt ye tool kaise madad karta hai?

Aap raqam darj karein aur 'Rupee Currency' checkbox check karein, ye foran 'ایک لاکھ پچیس ہزار روپے فقط' tayyar kar deta hai.

Ye tool kitni hadd tak numbers convert karta hai?

Ye tool sifar (0) se lekar 9,999,999 (ninnanway lakh ninnanway hazar nau sau ninnanway) tak poori accuracy ke sath convert karta hai.

Kya Roman Urdu mein bhi talaffuz milta hai?

Jee haan, Urdu Nastaliq rasm-ul-khat ke bilkul neeche Roman English mein bhi talaffuz likha aata hai.

Pakistani number system (Lakh/Hazar) use hota hai ya Million?

Ye tool mukammal tor par Pakistani aur sub-continental numbering system (Lakh, Hazar, Sau) ke mutabiq kaam karta hai.
