QR Code Generator Ki Mukammal Wazahat

Aaj kal ki digital dunya mein QR (Quick Response) codes har jagah istemal hote hain — restaurant ke menus se lekar visiting cards, business flyers, shop banners aur payments tak. Aik simple QR code kisi ko bhi lamba link type karne ki zahmat se bachata hai kyunke mobile camera foran scan kar ke mutalliqah page par le jata hai.

AshiKit QR Code Generator high error-correction technology par mabni static QR codes banata hai. Is ka matlab ye hai ke ye codes hamesha ke liye rehte hain, kabhi expire nahi hote aur scan karne par foran khul jate hain. Aap apne business card ke liye ya social media profiles ke liye transparent ya custom background ke sath PNG download kar sakte hain.
